## 2. Media Hosting & Content Policy

- **No Media Stored or Hosted:** Lyra Music does **NOT** host, store, archive, broadcast, upload, or transmit any audio, video, or copyright-protected media files on any servers or within this code repository.
- **Client-Side Architecture:** The application acts exclusively as a local media player frontend and protocol client. When playing content, media streams are requested directly by the client device from publicly available third-party servers.
- **No DRM Circumvention:** Lyra Music does not circumvent, bypass, decrypt, or impair any technological protection measures or Digital Rights Management (DRM) mechanisms. The application only accesses publicly exposed APIs and playback streams provided by YouTube for regular browser and mobile device playback in accordance with interoperability exceptions under 17 U.S.C. § 1201.

4. **Good Faith Statement:** A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
